# Start staking

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, hit the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_1 (16).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Earning options screen, choose the token you want to stake by searching the list or typing the token name on the search bar.

_In this example, we want to stake VARA tokens._

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_3 (22).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_4 (16).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have previously staked funds on the account, after choosing the "**Earning**" tab, you will be directed to the Your earning positions screen. From there, tap the "**+**" icon at the upper right corner to get to the Earning options screen.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_4 (10).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 3**: In the VARA earning options screen, select "**Direct nomination"** as your earning typ&#x65;**.**

{% hint style="info" %}
With other tokens, you won't need to perform this action.
{% endhint %}



<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_5 (17).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

A pop-up will appear. Read carefully, scroll down, then choose "**Stake to earn**" to proceed.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_6 (19).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (21).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

You will be directed to the following Start earning screen.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you have more than the minimum amount required for staking in your transferable balance to start staking and earning.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Enter the required earning information.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (20).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>Tips on selecting validator(s)</summary>

We suggest you pay close attention to the validator(s) you choose. When selecting a validator, SubWallet supports you with the latest record of its details.&#x20;

Click the three-dot icon on each validator's right side to see the details.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_9 (12).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_10 (13).png" alt="" data-size="original">

#### Find the most suitable validator(s)

You can use the **Sort** function to find the most suitable validator that fits your needs. Please click the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_159 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon at the top right corner and choose your sorting criteria. Once done, hit "**Reset sorting**".

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (25).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_12 (15).png" alt="" data-size="original">

</details>

A completed earning request will look like the following picture. Hit "**Stake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_13 (17).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, in addition to the above information, you will also need to select the staking account.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_16 (17).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_17 (13).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for rewards claims, unstaking, or withdrawal.
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: A popup will appear, asking you whether to continue with your chosen validators or go back to choose a different set of validators. Hit "**Continue**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6**: Check the information and confirm your staking request by pressing "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (15).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7:** Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_18 (14).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either select "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_19 (13).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", click the Earning tab as per Step 1. In the Your earning positions screen, tap your newly staked funds.
