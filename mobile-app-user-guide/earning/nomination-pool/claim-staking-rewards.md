# Claim staking rewards

### **Claiming options**

Once you receive rewards for staking in an active pool, you will need to claim them manually. With the rewards, you will have 2 options to claim:

<table><thead><tr><th width="349">Claiming options</th><th>Reference</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Stake into your chosen pool</td><td><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_125 (1).png" alt=""></td></tr><tr><td>Add to your transferable balance</td><td><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_124 (1).png" alt=""></td></tr></tbody></table>

{% hint style="success" %}
We recommend claiming staking rewards every 2 weeks to minimize paying gas fees each time you claim.
{% endhint %}

### **How to claim**

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, hit the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen. In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds for which you want to claim rewards.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (14).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2**: Select "**Claim rewards**" to start the claiming process.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_9 (7).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can also use the Notifications feature to perform this type of transaction.

From the homepage, press the <img src="https://docs.subwallet.app/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F631687399-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252F-Lh39Kwxa1xxZM9WX_Bs%252Fuploads%252FABMVKpycPXFreIIe1LiG%252FScreenshot_287.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D32cba28e-5292-496a-87d7-1fa00c0a8979&#x26;width=40&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=d2eee267&#x26;sv=2" alt="" data-size="line"> button at the top right of the screen. In the Notifications screen, look for the notification related to withdrawing tokens, then tap on that notification.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_30 (9).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_31 (10).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 3:** Hit "**Continue**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_10 (10).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "All accounts" mode, you will need to select the account for which you want to claim rewards.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (21).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_12 (10).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by hitting "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_13 (14).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either select "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you press "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (10).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}
