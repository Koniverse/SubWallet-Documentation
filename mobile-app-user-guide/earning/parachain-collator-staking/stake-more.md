# Stake more

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, tap the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen. In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds you want to stake more.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_1 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_17 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2**: Press "**Stake more**" to add more funds to your stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_19 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Enter the amount you want to add to your stake. Once done, hit "**Stake**".

{% hint style="info" %}
You can choose any collator as you add more funds to your stake.
{% endhint %}

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_20 (16).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_21 (16).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for rewards claims, unstaking, or withdrawal.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by hitting "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_22 (10).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_25 (15).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either select "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you press "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_26 (10).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", repeat the action as per Step 1. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then select your newly added staked funds.
