# Start staking

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, hit the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_1 (11).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Earning options screen, choose the token you want to stake by searching the list or typing the token name on the search bar.

_In this example, we want to stake DOT via Bifrost liquid staking._

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_3 (18).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_4 (12).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have previously staked funds on the account, after choosing the "**Earning**" tab, you will be directed to the Your earning positions screen. From there, press the "**+**" icon at the upper right corner to get to the Earning options screen.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_2 (14).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 3**: In the DOT earning options screen, select the "**DOT (Bifrost Polkadot)"** option as your liquid staking metho&#x64;**.**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_5 (14).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

A pop-up will appear. Read carefully, scroll down, then choose "**Stake to earn**" to proceed.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_6 (16).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (17).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 4**: Enter the amount you want to stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (15).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, in addition to the above information, you will also need to select the staking account.
{% endhint %}

Press "**Stake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_9 (8).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Choose the appropriate tab based on whether your tokens are on the required network:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="If your tokens are on the required network" %}
**Step 5**: Check the information and confirm your minting request by hitting "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (22).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6**: Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (13).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either select "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you press "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history, along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (11).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", hit the Earning tab as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then tap on your newly staked funds.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_16 (13).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_17 (10).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="If your tokens aren't on the required network" %}
{% hint style="info" %}
You need to have more than 1.5 DOT on the Polkadot network to be able to cross-chain them to Bifrost Polkadot and pay the gas fee for staking (if you don't have BNC).
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: A confirmation screen will appear, asking you to approve the cross-chain transaction from Polkadot to Bifrost Polkadot. Choose "**Approve**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_13 (15).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6**: You will be redirected to the Earning screen and then to the Mint screen. Check the information and confirm your minting request by hitting "**Approve**".&#x20;

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_12 (11).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (22).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 7**: Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (13).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either select "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you press "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history, along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (11).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", hit the Earning tab as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly staked funds.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_16 (13).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_17 (10).png" alt="" width="272"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
