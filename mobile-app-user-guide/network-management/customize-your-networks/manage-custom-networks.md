# Manage custom networks

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, tap the list item at the top left corner of the screen to get to the Settings section.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_21 (6).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Settings section, choose "**Manage networks**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_77 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Scroll down the network list or type the name to find your custom network.

_In this example, we will manage the "**Zora**" network - an EVM network._

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_9 (2).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Depending on what suits your needs, choose the appropriate tab:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Edit network information" %}
{% hint style="warning" %}
Once a network is imported onto SubWallet, you can only edit its information by:

* Adding another network's provider URL
* Adding the network's block explorer
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Tap the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_67 (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon to get to the Network detail screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_10 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5**: In the Network detail screen, add another provider URL or add the network's block explorer.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To add another provider URL, tap the "**+**" button at the upper right corner of the screen, then add your selected provider URL. Once done, hit "**Save**".

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (13).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_12 (5).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_13 (4) (1).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To add the network's block explorer, enter the corresponding explorer's site in the **Block explorer** field. Once done, hit "**Save**".

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_5 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Remove custom networks" %}
**Step 4**: Find the network you wish to remove and disable the toggle next to its name.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_1 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_2 (2).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 5**: Hit the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_67 (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon to get to the Network detail screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_16 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6**: In the Network detail section, press the bin icon at the top right corner of the screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_6 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7**: A pop-up screen will appear. Choose "**Delete**" to confirm the removal.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You've successfully removed the network! It won't be found in the network list anymore.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
