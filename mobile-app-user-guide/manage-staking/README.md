# Earning

### What is SubWallet Earning?

The Earning feature in SubWallet empowers you to maximize the potential of your assets within the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems, offering streamlined access to staking and other earning opportunities tailored to your portfolio.

We aggregate the best earning options from our partner protocols so you can easily compare, make informed decisions, and participate directly on a single interface.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Even though we try our best to select and display reliable earning options, as well as provide sufficient information with an error-tolerant UX, these protocols contain inherent risks that are out of our control. You should always do your own research before participating in any risk-bearing activities. SubWallet is not responsible for any loss of assets.
{% endhint %}
