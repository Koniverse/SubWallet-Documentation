# Create a new account with new seed phrase

### How SubWallet generates seed phrase for each account <a href="#how-subwallet-generates-seed-phrase-for-each-account" id="how-subwallet-generates-seed-phrase-for-each-account"></a>

When you create a new account on SubWallet, a 12-word phrase (also known as a **seed phrase/recovery phrase**) is generated. This phrase is essential for safeguarding your account and the assets held within the account.

{% hint style="info" %}
The seed phrase is randomly generated and consists of a series of 12 words taken from the [BIP-39 wordlist](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039/english.txt). With a 12-word seed phrase out of 2048 possible words, determining the right words in the correct sequence is almost impossible 😉
{% endhint %}

### Create a new account after successfully installing SubWallet <a href="#create-a-new-account-after-successfully-installing-subwallet" id="create-a-new-account-after-successfully-installing-subwallet"></a>

**Step 1**: After installing SubWallet, open the app and tap "**Create a new account**".

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_5 (6).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: Make sure you read all the Terms of Use by hitting the scroll-down button and agree with them by ticking the box beside "**I understand and agree to the Terms of Use, which apply to my use of SubWallet and all of its feature**". Once done, click "**Continue**".

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_6 (6).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (5).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 3:** Enter a strong password with at least 6 characters and tick "**I understand that SubWallet can't recover the password**". Once done, click "**Continue**".

{% hint style="info" %}
Password should contain at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character.
{% endhint %}

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (5).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_9 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 4**: Keep your seed phrase safe by tapping all the checkboxes to confirm you understand the importance of your seed phrase, then click "**Continue**".

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_10 (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_11 (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Anyone with access to your seed phrase can gain control over your funds.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If, for instance, you forget your password, you will need your seed phrase to import the account again and set up a new password.
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: Your seed phrase will be shown to you. Select "**I have kept it somewhere safe**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_13 (1) (2) (1).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you want to store seed your phrase, you can either:

* Select "**Copy to clipboard**" to paste it, or
* Choose "**Download seed phrase**" to save it as a file downloaded from your browser.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_12 (1) (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 5:** Verify the seed phrase by putting each word in the phrase in its right order. If all the words are in the right order, tap "**Finish**" to proceed.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_14 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_15 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 6**: Enter a name for your newly created account. Once done, hit "**Continue**".

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_23 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="275"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7**: Your account has been successfully set up. Tap "**Go to home**" to get to the homepage.

### Create a new account while using SubWallet

**Step 1**: Open the SubWallet app and tap on the account name to access the account selection tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_16 (7).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the account selection tab, choose "**Create a new account**", then hit "**Create with a new seed phrase**".

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_17 (4).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_18 (5).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 3**: Keep your seed phrase in a safe place by hitting "**Copy to clipboard**" or "**Download seed phrase**". Then, tap "**I have kept it somewhere safe**".

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_19 (1) (2) (1).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_20 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 4:** Verify the seed phrase by putting each word in the phrase in its right order. If all the words are in the right order, tap "**Finish**" to proceed.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_21 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_22 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 5**: Enter a name for your newly created account. Once done, hit "**Continue**".

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_23 (1) (2).png" alt="" width="275"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be redirected to the homepage, where you can see your newly created account.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_24 (4).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_25 (5).png" alt="" width="270"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="info" %}
For each seed phrase generated with the SubWallet extension, a unified account will be created and used to manage assets and make transactions across all ecosystems.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Note that in order to see your assets, you will need to manually enable the networks you want to use & have assets on.

Follow [this instruction](https://docs.subwallet.app/main/extension-user-guide/network-management/customize-your-networks/enable-disable-networks) to enable the networks you want to use.
{% endhint %}
