# Enable/disable tokens

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, tap the list item at the top left of the screen to get to the Settings section.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_21 (7).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Settings screen, choose "**Manage tokens**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_77 (4).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Select the token you want to enable/disable by scrolling down the list or typing its name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_78 (3).png" alt="" width="282"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then, enable/disable the toggle next to the token name.
