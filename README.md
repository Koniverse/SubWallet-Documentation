# Introduction

## About SubWallet

SubWallet is the comprehensive non-custodial wallet solution for Polkadot, Ethereum, TON, Cardano & Bitcoin ecosystems.&#x20;

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xY9NyrqMY6E" %}

Built on top of Polkadot {.js}, SubWallet focuses on improving UX & UI. We envision a crypto wallet as a Web3 multiverse gateway through which users can enjoy multi-chain services with utmost ease and absolute security.&#x20;

Connecting and using blockchain-based applications in Polkadot, Ethereum, TON, Cardano & Bitcoin ecosystems is smoother than ever with SubWallet Browser Extension & SubWallet Mobile App (Android & iOS).&#x20;

## SubWallet Crypto Wallet Key Features

1. Manage multi-chain assets on 200+ networks in Polkadot, Ethereum, TON, Cardano & Bitcoin ecosystems with 450+ tokens supported
2. Manage multiple seed phrases for Polkadot, Ethereum, TON, Cardano & Bitcoin accounts with only one master password
3. Manage assets & perform transactions across Polkadot, Ethereum, TON, Cardano & Bitcoin ecosystems with one single **unified account**
4. Send & receive assets cross-chain in Polkadot, Kusama & Ethereum ecosystems
5. Swap tokens (both single-chain & cross-chain) in 1 click across Polkadot & Ethereum ecosystems with optimal routes and minimal fees
6. Display & manage NFT
7. Stake to earn in-app easily via direct nomination, nomination pools, collator staking, liquid staking & subnet staking (Polkadot, Kusama, Aleph Zero, Vara Network, Mythos, Bifrost, Moonbeam, Bittensor, etc.)
8. Explore Web3 apps without friction
9. Sync desktop & mobile wallets within seconds
10. Boost security with hardware crypto wallets Ledger & Keystone, as well as Polkadot Vault (previously Parity QR-Signer)
11. Buy crypto from fiat using your credit & debit card \
    And a lot more!
