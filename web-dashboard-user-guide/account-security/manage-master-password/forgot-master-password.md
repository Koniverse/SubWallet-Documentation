# Forgot master password

{% hint style="info" %}
If you forgot your password, don't worry! You can quickly regain access to your accounts by re-importing them using seed phrase/JSON backup file/Private key/QR backup code, and you'll be back in control in no time.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1**: On the Welcome screen, click "**Don't remember your password?**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (28) (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:** A pop-up message will appear with 2 options provided: "**Reset account**" and "**Erase all**".

Choose the option you want to apply:

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (29) (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### **With the "Reset account" option**

{% hint style="info" %}
This action will **reset some** information associated with your account, including your:

* Balances & NFTs
* Earning
* Crowdloans
* Transactions (History)
* Address book
* Website access config/settings
{% endhint %}

#### **With the "Erase all" option**

{% hint style="info" %}
This action will **reset all** the information associated with your account.

Besides resetting the information listed like when you click "**Reset account**", it will also reset your:

* List of custom networks and tokens
* Network & asset state (turn on/turn off)
* Settings: security, currency, language, ...
{% endhint %}

**Step 3**: Once you click either of these options, all the accounts you have previously imported will be removed. You will be directed to the Welcome screen.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (30) (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In this step, you will need to re-import your account using your backup information.
