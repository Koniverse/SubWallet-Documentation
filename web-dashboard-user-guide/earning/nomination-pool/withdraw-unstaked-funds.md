# Withdraw unstaked funds

### **Track the withdrawal time**

After successfully unstaking your tokens, you must wait for the unstaking process to finish before you can withdraw and make them transferable.

{% hint style="info" %}
The unstaking period varies depending on each token and network you've previously staked on.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2241).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds for which you've previously unstaked.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2242).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: In the Earning position details screen, click the "**Withdraw details**" button on the Withdraw infỏ field to see the countdown for the withdrawal.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2243).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2244).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Withdraw your funds

{% hint style="info" %}
**On**ce the unstaking process ends, you need to withdraw your funds manually to make them transferable.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2246).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds for which you've previously unstaked.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2245).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: In the Earning position details screen, click the "**Withdraw**" button under the Withdraw info section to start the withdrawal.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2247).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4**: A popup screen will appear on the right side. Click "**Continue**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2248).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to select the account you want to withdraw.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2250).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2251).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2249).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2252).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2253).png>)
{% endhint %}
