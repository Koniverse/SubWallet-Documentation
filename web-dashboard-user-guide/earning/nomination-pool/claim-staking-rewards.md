# Claim staking rewards

### **Claiming options**

Once you receive rewards for staking in an active pool, you will need to claim them manually. With the rewards, you will have 2 options to claim:

<table><thead><tr><th width="349" align="center">Claiming options</th><th align="center" valign="middle">Reference</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td align="center">Stake into your chosen pool</td><td align="center" valign="middle"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_125 (1).png" alt=""></td></tr><tr><td align="center">Add to your transferable balance</td><td align="center" valign="middle"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_124 (1).png" alt=""></td></tr></tbody></table>

{% hint style="success" %}
We recommend claiming staking rewards every 2 weeks to avoid paying a gas fee each time you claim.
{% endhint %}

### **How to claim**

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2239).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions screen, click the "**Claim rewards**" button on the funds you want to claim.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2223).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** A popup screen will appear on the right side. Click "**Continue**" to proceed.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you don't want to stake your rewards, don't tick the "Stake reward after claim" box.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2224).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "All accounts" mode, you will need to select the account for which you want to claim rewards.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2225).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2227).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2229).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2230).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2231).png>)
{% endhint %}
