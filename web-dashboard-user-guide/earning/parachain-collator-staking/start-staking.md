# Start staking

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2575).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Earning options screen, choose the token you want to stake by searching the list or typing the token name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2028).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_In this example, we want to stake DEV tokens on Moonbase Alpha. Once you've found the token, click on the token's box._

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2576).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have previously staked funds on the account, after choosing the "**Earning**" tab, you will be directed to the Your earning positions screen. From there, click the "**+**" button or the "Add more funds" button to get to the Earning options screen.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2159).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 3**: A pop-up will appear. Read carefully, scroll down, then choose "**Stake to earn**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2577).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be directed to the following Start earning screen.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you have more than the minimum amount required for staking in your transferable balance to start staking and earning.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Enter the required earning information.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2514).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

* [ ] **Tip #1:**&#x20;

We suggest you pay close attention to the collator you are choosing. SubWallet supports you with the latest record of collator details.&#x20;

Click the three-dot icon on each collator's right side to see the details.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2516).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

</details>

A completed earning request will look like the following picture. Click "**Stake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2523).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, in addition to the above information, you will also need to select the staking account.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2522).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2520).png>)
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for rewards claims, unstaking, or withdrawal.
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: A popup screen will appear on the right side. Check the information and confirm your staking request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2524).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6:** Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2525).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2526).png>)
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, click the Earning tab as per _**Step 1**_. In the Your earning positions screen, click on your newly staked funds.
