# Unstake

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2204).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions screen, click "**Unstake**" on the funds you want to start the unstaking process.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2205).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** A popup screen will appear on the right side. Enter the amount & the collator you want to unstake, then click "**Unstake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2206).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to first select the account for which you want to unstake.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2207).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2208).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2209).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2210).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2211).png>)
{% endhint %}
