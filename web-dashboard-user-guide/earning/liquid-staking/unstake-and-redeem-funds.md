# Unstake & redeem funds

### Unstaking process & its characteristics

With liquid staking, once you have successfully unstaked your minted token, your redeemed funds will be in the unstaking queue. This means the funds will only arrive in your account once it's your turn.

Depending on the market conditions (particularly the liquidity), your turn can come sooner or later than expected, but it won't exceed the maximum unstaking period (28 days for DOT, 7 days for MANTA).&#x20;

You can speed up the process by choosing to unstake immediately (i.e., "_lightning mode_" on the Bifrost app or "_Fast unstake_" in SubWallet). Although this option is much faster (as you won't have to wait for the unstaking period), the funds you redeem will be less than the normal unstake option, as you have to pay a more significant fee to get your funds ahead of others in the unstaking queue.

### Unstake & redeem your funds

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2266).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions option screen, click the "Unstake" button on the funds you want to unstake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2283).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** A popup screen will appear on the right side. Enter the amount you want to unstake, then click "**Unstake**" to proceed.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you wish to unstake a portion of your minted tokens, ensure the remaining amount you continue to stake is higher than the minimum required active stake; otherwise, you won't be able to perform the action.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2284).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can fasten up the unstaking process by ticking the "**Fast Unstake**" field. Once the transaction is made, you can redeem your funds immediately.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2286).png>)
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to select the account you want to unstake.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2287).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2288).png>)
{% endhint %}

&#x20;**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2289).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
With "**Fast Unstake**" you'll see a field labeled "**Minimum receivables**" on the confirmation screen. In situations where liquidity is low, you might receive a low redemption quote. SubWallet safeguards you by enforcing a minimum receivables threshold. The transaction will not be processed if the amount you are trying to redeem is lower than this threshold.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2290).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2291).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

### Withdraw unstaked xcDOT <a href="#withdraw-unstaked-xcdot" id="withdraw-unstaked-xcdot"></a>

{% hint style="info" %}
This action is needed only in StellaSwap liquid staking (xcDOT staking). After choosing to unstake normally, you will need to wait **up to 28 days** for your turn in the unstaking queue. Note that the wait time is not fixed and may vary depending on the current queue status.

Once it's your turn, you need to withdraw the funds manually to redeem xcDOT.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_175 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions option screen, select the funds you want to withdraw.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_176 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: In the Earning position details screen, click the "**Withdraw**" button on the Withdraw info to start the withdrawal.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_177 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4:** A popup screen will appear on the right side. Click "**Continue**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_178 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to select the account you want to withdraw.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_180 (2).png" alt="" data-size="original"> ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_181 (1).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 5**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_179 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6:** Your request has been submitted!

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will display the latest transaction record in your transaction history, along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.
{% endhint %}
