# Liquid staking

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/shorts/0wkMWus_Dcs" fullWidth="false" %}

## Earning information & parameters for supported liquid staking options

### &#x20;1. Acala liquid staking

<table><thead><tr><th width="184">Parameter</th><th>Information</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Minimum stake</td><td>1 DOT (Acala) or 2 DOT (Polkadot)<sup>1</sup></td></tr><tr><td>Minted token</td><td><p><mark style="color:$success;"><strong>LDOT</strong></mark></p><ul><li>LDOT stands for Liquid DOT, a yield-bearing token representing the staked DOT and the staking yield continuously accruing</li><li>The amount of LDOT doesn’t increase over time; only its value in DOT does</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Unstake options</td><td><ul><li>Fast unstake: withdraw your DOT instantly with a higher fee</li><li>Normal unstake: wait up to 28 days to withdraw with a lower fee</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Stake &#x26; unstake fees</td><td><ul><li>Stake: fees will be paid either in ACA or DOT</li><li>Unstake: fees will be paid in ACA</li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>

<sup>_1_</sup>_&#x20;If you only have DOT on Polkadot, make sure you have more than 2 DOT to be able to cross-chain them to Acala and pay the gas fee for staking._

### 2. Bifrost liquid staking

#### 2.1. DOT

<table><thead><tr><th width="183">Parameter</th><th>Information</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Minimum stake</td><td>0.5 DOT (Bifrost Polkadot) or 1.5 DOT (Polkadot)<sup>2</sup></td></tr><tr><td>Minted token</td><td><p><mark style="color:$success;"><strong>vDOT</strong></mark></p><ul><li>vDOT stands for voucher DOT, a yield-bearing token representing the staked DOT plus the continuously accruing staking yield</li><li>The amount of vDOT doesn’t increase over time; only its value in DOT does</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Unstake options</td><td><ul><li>Fast unstake: withdraw your DOT instantly with a higher fee</li><li>Normal unstake: wait up to 28 days to withdraw with a lower fee</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Stake &#x26; unstake fees</td><td><ul><li>Stake: fees will be paid either in BNC or DOT</li><li>Unstake: fees will be paid in BNC</li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>

<sup>_2_</sup>_&#x20;If you only have DOT on Polkadot, make sure you have more than 1.5 DOT to be able to cross-chain them to Bifrost Polkadot and pay the gas fee for staking._

#### 2.2. MANTA

<table><thead><tr><th width="183">Parameter</th><th>Information</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Minimum stake</td><td>1 MANTA (Bifrost Polkadot)<sup>3</sup></td></tr><tr><td>Minted token</td><td><p><mark style="color:$success;"><strong>vMANTA</strong></mark></p><ul><li>vMANTA stands for voucher MANTA, a yield-bearing token representing the staked MANTA plus the continuously accruing staking yield</li><li>The amount of vMANTA doesn’t increase over time; only its value in MANTA does</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Unstake options</td><td><ul><li>Fast unstake: withdraw your MANTA instantly with a higher fee</li><li>Normal unstake: wait up to 7 days to withdraw with a lower fee</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Stake &#x26; unstake fees</td><td><ul><li>Stake: fees will be paid either in BNC or MANTA</li><li>Unstake: fees will be paid in BNC</li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>

<sup>_3_</sup> _Cross-chain MANTA between Manta Atlantic and Bifrost Polkadot is disabled on Oct 31, 2025, as part of the vMANTA 2.0 migration timeline. Check out this_ [_article_](https://www.reddit.com/r/Polkadot/s/Bkc0vqSf84) _for further details._\
_If you already have MANTA/vMANTA on Bifrost Polkadot, you won't need to take any action. The migration process is automated._

### 3. StellaSwap liquid staking

<table><thead><tr><th width="181">Parameter</th><th>Information</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Minimum stake</td><td>0 xcDOT (Moonbeam)<sup>4</sup></td></tr><tr><td>Minted token</td><td><mark style="color:$success;"><strong>stDOT</strong></mark>: a liquid token representing your share of the total xcDOT pool deposited with StellaSwap.</td></tr><tr><td>Unstake time</td><td><ul><li>Fast unstake: unstake withdraw your xcDOT instantly with a higher fee</li><li>Normal unstake: wait up to 28 days to withdraw with a lower fee. You will need to withdraw manually to complete the process</li></ul></td></tr><tr><td>Stake &#x26; unstake fees</td><td>Fees will be paid in GLMR</td></tr></tbody></table>

<sup>_4_</sup>_&#x20;If you only have DOT on Polkadot, you need to cross-chain them to Moonbeam first, then stake._
