# Start staking

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2266).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Earning options screen, choose the token you want to stake by searching the list or typing the token name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2267).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have previously staked funds on the account, after choosing the "**Earning**" tab, you will be directed to the Your earning positions screen. From there, click the "**+**" button or the "Add more funds" button to get to the Earning options screen.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2159).png>)
{% endhint %}

_In this example, we want to stake DOT tokens on Bifrost Polkadot. Select "DOT" to start._

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2268).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**_:_ In the DOT earning options screen, select one of the three "**Liquid staking"** options as your earning typ&#x65;**.**

_In this example, we want to stake DOT via Bifrost liquid staking._

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2269).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4**: A pop-up will appear. Read carefully, scroll down, then choose "**Stake to earn**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2270).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5**: Enter the amount you want to stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2271).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "All accounts" mode, in addition to the above information, you will also need to select the staking account.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2272).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2274).png>)
{% endhint %}

Once done, click "**Stake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2281).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Choose the appropriate tab based on whether your tokens are on the required network (in this case, i.e., DOT amount is higher or lower DOT on Bifrost):

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="If your tokens are on the required network" %}
**Step 6**: A popup screen will appear on the right side. Check the information and confirm your minting request by clicking "**Approve**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2278).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7**: Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2279).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history, along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2280).png>)
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", click the Earning tab as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly staked funds.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="If your tokens aren't on the required networkd Tab" %}
{% hint style="warning" %}
You need to have more than 1.5 DOT on the Polkadot network to be able to cross-chain them to Bifrost Polkadot and pay the gas fee for staking (if you don't have BNC).
{% endhint %}

**Step 6**: A confirmation screen will appear on the right side, asking you to approve the cross-chain transaction from Polkadot to Bifrost Polkadot and to mint vDOT on Bifrost Polkadot afterwards. Choose "**Approve**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2282).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 7**: Your staking request is submitted. You can either click "**View progress**" to view the transaction progress in the Notifications screen or "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage.

{% hint style="info" %}
DO NOT close this view until the transaction is complete. Closing it before completion will result in the transaction failing.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_101 (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the transaction is complete, you can check the status of your staked funds by clicking the Earning tab as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly staked funds.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
