# Unstake & redeem TAO

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2575).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Your earning positions screen, click "**Unstake**" to add more funds to your stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2331).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: A popup screen will appear on the right side. Enter the amount and the validator you want to unstake.&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
Like staking, there will be an inherent loss of value during the unstaking action (also known as **slippage**).

If the slippage calculated by SubWallet for your transaction exceeds 0.5%, you will not be able to proceed with your stake. In this case, you will need to adjust your slippage tolerance to a higher percentage than what we calculated to proceed.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2467).png>)

To adjust the slippage, click the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_67 (6).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon next to the slippage, then select or enter your desired slippage tolerance and click "**Apply**".

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2315).png>)
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2332).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once done, click "**Unstake**".

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2468).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2469).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
An unstaking fee of 0.00005 TAO will be deducted from your stake once the transaction is complete.&#x20;

_For example, if you unstake 1 TAO, after the transaction is complete, you will redeem 1 - 0.00005, which equals 0.99995 TAO._

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1) (1) (1).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2470).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2471).png>)
{% endhint %}

Once the transaction is complete, you will instantly receive TAO.
