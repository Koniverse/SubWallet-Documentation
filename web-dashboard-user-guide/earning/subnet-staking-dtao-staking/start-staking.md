# Start staking

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**Earning**" tab on the sidebar.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2575).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: In the Earning options screen, choose the token you want to stake by searching the list or typing the token name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2028).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_In this example, we want to stake TAO tokens on Bittensor Testnet. Once you've found the token, click on the token's box._

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2301).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have previously staked funds on the account, after choosing the "**Earning**" tab, you will be directed to the Your earning positions screen. From there, click the "**+**" icon at the upper right corner to get to the Earning options screen.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2302).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 3**: In the TAO earning options screen, choose the subnet you want to stake by searching the list or typing the subnet name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2303).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2304).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

A pop-up will appear. Read carefully, scroll down, then choose "**Stake to earn**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2305).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be directed to the following Start earning screen.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you have more than the minimum amount required for staking in your transferable balance to start staking and earning.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Enter the required earning information. That includes:

* Staking amount
* Validator
* Slippage (if the minimum slippage tolerance is higher than the default one)

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2306).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, in addition to the above information, you will also need to select the staking account.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2307).png>) ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2308).png>)
{% endhint %}

<details>

We suggest you pay close attention to the validator you are choosing. When selecting a validator, SubWallet supports you with the latest record of validator details.&#x20;

Click the three-dot icon on each validator's right side to see the details.

You can use the **Sort** function to find the most suitable validator that fits your needs. Please click the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_159 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon at the top right corner and choose your sorting criteria.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2312).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>Set slippage tolerance for the transaction</summary>

When staking on any subnet (alpha), there is an inherent loss of value during the transaction called **slippage**. SubWallet will calculate this number once you enter the stake amount and validator.

{% hint style="info" %}
The default slippage tolerance SubWallet sets for any staking action is 0.5%.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the slippage calculated by SubWallet for your transaction exceeds 0.5%, you will not be able to proceed with your stake. In this case, you will need to adjust your slippage tolerance to a higher percentage than what we calculated to proceed.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2314).png>)

To adjust the slippage, click the <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_67 (6).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon next to the slippage, then select or enter your desired slippage tolerance and click "**Apply**".

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2315).png>)
{% endhint %}

</details>

A completed earning request will look like the following picture. Click "**Stake**" to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2316).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5**: A popup screen will appear on the right side. Check the information and confirm your staking request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2317).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
A staking fee of 0.00005 TAO will be deducted from your stake once the transaction is complete.&#x20;

_For example, if you enter 1 TAO in the Amount field, after the transaction is complete, your staked amount will be 1 - 0.00005, which equals 0.99995 TAO._

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (54).png>)
{% endhint %}

**Step 6:** Your staking request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2318).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2319).png>)
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", click the Earning tab as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly staked funds.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (2320).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
