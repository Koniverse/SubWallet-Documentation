# Show/hide balances

{% hint style="info" %}
SubWallet will hide your balances by default when you first use the extension.
{% endhint %}

You can choose to either unveil your balances to get a clear view of your assets or keep them private from prying eyes, safeguarding your funds from public exposure.

### To show your balances

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, click the  <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_90 (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon to unveil your balance.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2154).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: Your balances will be displayed once you click.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2155).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### To hide your balances

**Step 1**: On the SubWallet homepage, click the <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_91 (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon to hide your balance.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2156).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: All information regarding your balances will be hidden once you click.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2157).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
While hidden, you can still view your balances individually by clicking on the token you want to unveil.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (2158).png>)
{% endhint %}
