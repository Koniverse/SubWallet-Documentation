# Manage dApps

{% hint style="success" %}
Users can **view, search, and bookmark** their favorite dApps in the Web dashboard interface.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### View a dApp

On the SubWallet homepage, select the "**dApps**" tab on the sidebar.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2109).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

In the dApps screen, you will see a list of dApps supported on SubWallet. When you click on the dApp(s), you will be redirected to the dApp(s)' websites.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2110).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

We have also **categorized the dApps** into various categories, which you can view by selecting the tabs here.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2111).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Bookmark a dApp

To **bookmark a dApp as a favorite**, click the <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_96 (4).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon on that dApp.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2112).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

When a dApp is bookmarked as a favorite, it will be prioritized and displayed at the top of the page.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2113).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Search for a dApp

Additionally, you can **search for a dApp** by entering the dApp name in the search bar.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (2114).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
You can also filter out the dApp(s) you want to see by clicking the <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_41 (6).png" alt="" data-size="line"> icon on the search bar.

A popup screen will appear on the right side, showcasing the supported dApp types. Choose the ones you want to apply, then hit "**Apply filter**".

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (2116).png>)
{% endhint %}
