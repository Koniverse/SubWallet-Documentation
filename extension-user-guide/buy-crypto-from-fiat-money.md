# Buy crypto from fiat money

{% hint style="info" %}
SubWallet has partnered with Transak, Banxa, and Coinbase Pay to provide users with on-ramp services.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### If you are in Single-account mode

**Step 1**: Open the SubWallet extension. On the homepage, click the "**Buy**" icon.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_34 (2).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: Select the token and the corresponding supplier you want to buy from. There will be a list for you to choose from. Once done, click "**Buy now**".

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_35 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_36.png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 3:** Read the disclaimer carefully, then click "**Agree**" to proceed. SubWallet will redirect you to your chosen supplier's website so you can continue and complete the transaction.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_37.png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### If you are in "All accounts" mode

{% hint style="info" %}
In "**All accounts**" mode, most steps are identical to the "Single-account mode". The only additional step is to select the account for which you want to buy tokens.&#x20;

{% endhint %}
