# Mission Pools

{% hint style="info" %}
This feature allows you to participate in various live and upcoming airdrop campaigns on the Polkadot, TON & Ethereum ecosystem with just a few clicks. Grab yourself a chance to earn amazing rewards that will boost your balance significantly!
{% endhint %}

**Step 1:** Open the SubWallet extension and click on the "**Missions**" tab at the bottom of the screen.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_40 (1) (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**: The list of campaigns will appear. Select the campaign you want to join from the list.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_41 (1) (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
With each campaign, we provide all the information needed so you can decide whether to participate or not. This includes:

* Campaign name
* Supported network(s) for the campaign
* Status: Live or Archived
* Campaign type(s)
* Campaign's description
* Total rewards
* Timeline
* Total winners
* <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_6 (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line">: Project website
* <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_7 (1) (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="line">: Project Twitter (X) page.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_42 (1) (1).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_43 (1).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

Once you have selected a campaign, click "**Join now**" to participate.

You will be directed to the airdrop campaign platform. **Complete all the tasks and quests, and may the odds be ever in your favor!**
