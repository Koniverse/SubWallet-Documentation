# Unstake & redeem TAO

**Step 1**: Open the SubWallet extension and choose the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen. In the Your earning positions screen, select the subnet from which you want to unstake your funds.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_1 (28).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_25 (18).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2**: Click "**Stake more**" to add more funds to your stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_26 (13).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Enter the amount and the validator you want to unstake. Once done, click "Uns**take**".

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_27 (10).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_28 (11).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for unstaking.
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_29 (12).png" alt="" width="362"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1981).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.
