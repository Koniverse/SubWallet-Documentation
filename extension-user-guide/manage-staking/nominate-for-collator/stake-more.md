# Stake more

**Step 1**: Open the SubWallet extension and choose the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen. In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds you want to stake more.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_211 (2).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_205 (3).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2**: Click "**Stake more**" to add more funds to your stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_207 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Enter the amount and the collator you want to stake. Once done, click "**Stake**".

{% hint style="info" %}
You can choose to stake to another collator as you add more funds to your stake.&#x20;

Each collator will require a different minimum staking amount, so if you don't have enough tokens to meet that minimum, you can still stake more with your initially chosen collator as long as you have tokens in your transferable balance.
{% endhint %}

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_208 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_209 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for rewards claims, unstaking, or withdrawal.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to select the staking account first.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_202 (3).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_203 (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_210 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1981).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_212 (1).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", repeat the action as per Step 1. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly added staked funds.
