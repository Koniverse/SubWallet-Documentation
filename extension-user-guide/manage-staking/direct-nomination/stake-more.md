# Stake more

**Step 1**: Open the SubWallet extension and choose the "**Earning**" tab at the bottom of the screen. In the Your earning positions screen, select the funds you want to stake more.

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_160 (2).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_161 (2).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2**: Click "**Stake more**" to add more funds to your stake.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_162 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**: Enter the amount you want to add to your stake. Once done, click "**Stake**".

{% hint style="info" %}
Unlike earning via nomination pool, with direct nomination, you can choose another validator as you add more funds to your stake.
{% endhint %}

<div><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_163 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_166 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Make sure you leave enough tokens to pay gas fees for rewards claims, unstaking, or withdrawal.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If you are in the "**All accounts**" mode, you will need to select the staking account before entering an amount.

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_164 (2).png" alt="" data-size="original"><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_165 (1).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

**Step 4**: Check the information and confirm your request by clicking "**Approve**".&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_167 (1).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5:** Your request has been submitted!

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1981).png" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can either click "**Back to home**" to return to the homepage or "**View transaction**" to see transaction details in the History tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you click "**View transaction**", SubWallet will show you the latest transaction record in your transaction history along with the extrinsic hash of the transfer.&#x20;

<img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_168 (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endhint %}

To check the status of your staked funds, after selecting "**Back to home**", repeat the action as per **Step 1**. In the Your earning positions screen, scroll down, then click on your newly added staked funds.
